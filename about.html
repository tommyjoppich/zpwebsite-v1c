About

Where good ideas get tested, not just trusted.

Since 2019

Started with one working idea.

ZeroPlus Derivatives began in 2019 with a single mispriced pocket of the options market and a strategy built to capture it. That first idea held up, and the desk grew from there into a full set of systematic and discretionary strategies.

We don't carry much structure. Traders, engineers, and analysts all sit close enough to interrupt each other — which, most days, is exactly the point.

Our Approach

If the data doesn't hold up, neither does the trade.

We treat every strategy as a hypothesis first. Our process leans on evidence over instinct, running across all 18 US options exchanges on infrastructure we built ourselves — some of it automated, some of it run by hand, all of it tested before a single dollar trades.

Our Edge

Take any one of these away, and the model breaks.

01

Proprietary Infrastructure

We build and maintain every system we trade on ourselves, so a tested idea can go live in days.

02

No Bottlenecks

An idea gets tested on the evidence behind it, not the title of the person who had it.

03

Fully Self-Funded

Every strategy trades our own money, which keeps the incentive simple: be right, or stop.
